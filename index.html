AI + Operations

I use AI to increase human capability.

My interest in AI is operational: where can it reduce friction, improve consistency, accelerate analysis, make knowledge easier to use, or let a capable team handle more sophisticated work?

The goal is practical implementation with appropriate human judgment and oversight, not automation for its own sake.

Working with me

AI-Enabled Operations Diagnostic

I review the operating system behind the work: workflow bottlenecks, ownership, founder or executive dependency, reporting, knowledge management, repetitive manual work, process consistency, team capability, specialist bottlenecks, and practical AI opportunities.

The output is a prioritized operating plan showing what should be fixed, standardized, simplified, automated or AI-enabled, and what should remain human-led.
